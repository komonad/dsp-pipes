# Rendering

> This document is also available in [Chinese](RENDERING.md).

The item icon uses a centered orthographic render of a coaxial straight tube with identical end collars. Its length is about 2.1 times its collar diameter, and its silhouette spans 90% of the tile width. Translucent blue walls retain visible coverage in the 80-pixel inventory atlas. Black/white matting reconstructs straight-alpha pixels from the transparent render. `pwsh tests/run.ps1 -RenderIcon` regenerates `../screenshots/pipe-icon-orthographic.png` from the parametric model in `../tests/PipeIconRender.cs`; `assets-src/fluid-pipe.png` is the embedded source image. `pwsh tests/run.ps1 -IconOnly` checks the embedded icon and native atlas registration.

The valve has an independent item and recipe icon with a silver body, two glass ports and an orange handwheel. The same renderer exports `../screenshots/pipe-valve-icon-orthographic.png`; `assets-src/fluid-valve.png` is its embedded source. Both icon models share glass reflection exposure. `PipeVisuals` configures the world materials.

The world valve uses a procedural housing with a rounded axial body, beveled metal flanges, recessed fasteners, an integrated top actuator and a low mounting plinth. Its two ports sit 1.2 world units apart on a common centerline; cyan identifies side A and amber identifies side B. Port height matches the pipe centerline. Smooth radial normals preserve continuous cylinder reflections, while profile edges define the flange chamfers and open bores. Five native GPU material groups separate the shell, steel, panel and port markings. The model remains below the next transport tier. Native instance rendering, construction previews and collisions use the registered prototype geometry.

Procedural meshes follow the actual connections. The built-in fluid pipe has a diameter of 0.64 world units, with its centerline 0.34 units above the native transport anchor. Thirty-two-sided glass walls have an inward-facing surface at 92% of the outer radius. Collinear segments share a continuous transparent surface. Elbows and multi-branch junctions use opaque steel, with hollow, beveled cuffs where their branches meet adjoining tubes. Junctions use a smooth union of rounded tube shapes. Convex junctions inset their meeting point and curve the branches while preserving tangents at adjoining tubes. Blending fades at ray origins to preserve the outer radius. Free ends have rounded glass heads with steel retaining rims. An isolated node is a short transparent cylinder with two rounded heads; incident connection directions define its regenerated shape. Setting `Appearance.MetalFittings = false` in the BepInEx configuration selects transparent fitting surfaces after a game restart.

One pipe item and one shared glass-wall material serve every fluid. The internal fluid volume reads the stored item's native tank palette from `IconSet.itemDescArr`: each 40-float descriptor stores `IconDesc.reserved0` at offset 20. The tank shader's sRGB conversion and 1.5 exposure produce a linear HDR tint, uploaded in a one-pixel RGBAHalf texture. Palette changes replace the tint texture while preserving material and geometry ownership. Additional item types use the descriptor's face color when the tank palette is unset; missing descriptors use neutral gray. Empty networks show lightly tinted clear walls.

Pipe inspection renders the current node in green and direct neighbors in cyan through separate silhouette passes. The selected node uses the game's outline pass; an additional pass composites adjacent nodes with cloned native materials and the renderer's temporary buffers. The mask follows cached glass-wall, fitting and machine-socket geometry. Native downsampling and compositing provide the same contour treatment as other selected buildings. Closing the inspector releases its node meshes.

The wall uses the game's generic PBR glass shader with a lightly tinted wall, uniform high smoothness, specular reflection and the storage tank's rim tint. Lower per-surface opacity preserves visibility through both glass interfaces at grazing angles. Storage tank vertex animation depends on tank geometry; the generic glass shader accepts arbitrary pipe meshes. Steel fittings use the Standard metallic shader with 0.98 metalness and 0.86 smoothness. A shared 64-pixel HDR cubemap approximates sky, horizon and ground illumination, scoped to pipe draws through a material property block. The map is generated once per active factory from approximate environmental colors. Opaque fittings include inward-facing triangles with reversed normals so their interiors block the background when viewed through adjoining tube openings. The inner volume uses unlit transparent tint textures, keeping its color independent of surface orientation and sunlight. Shared boundary frames join adjoining glass and fluid rings. Glass interiors render before all exterior walls, including across spatial chunks, to preserve transparent layer order. Glass and fluid use unshadowed transparent passes; steel receives direct lighting and shadows. Standard materials provide wall fallbacks. A component containing fluid displays a uniform, full cross-section whose material is selected by item identity.

Meshes are cached by spatial chunk, registered pipe type and the extension's semantic group, with frustum culling. Inventory changes reuse the geometry and select materials per network. Connection changes rebuild affected outer chunks and invalidate cached fluid group meshes. The renderer submits cached meshes grouped by spatial chunk.

Registered types configure wall radius, center height, fitting proportions and optional support rings through `PipeGeometryProfile`. Shared boundary positions account for both nodes' center heights. At a mixed-radius boundary, the larger side supplies an opaque beveled reducer with an internal tapered lining. Collider bounds, selection meshes, machine sockets and inserter saddles follow the registered dimensions. The built-in fluid type retains the dimensions described above.

Custom dimensions adjust native building overlap queries to the pipe centerline, radius and standalone length. Changed nodes and their neighbors queue collider updates for the main thread; actual bounds changes invalidate the native collision proxies. A successful type upgrade also invalidates the next adjacency layer because a changed center height can move fitting ownership at a neighbor's far endpoint.

`PipeAppearance` assigns independently owned surface materials and an optional inner core. Its `textureCoordinate` constructor parameter accepts `Func<PlanetFactory, Vector3, float>`, mapping a non-null factory and world position to a finite longitudinal UV coordinate. Positions remain stable across spatial chunk boundaries. An omitted callback uses local arc length. Internal geometry previews without a factory also use local arc length and do not invoke the callback. Exceptions, NaN and infinity disable the type's coordinate callback and produce a diagnostic. Animated effects use a cleared `MaterialPropertyBlock` for each surface draw and preserve cached geometry. Texture resources supplied by an extension remain owned by that extension. Callback failures select cached fallback behavior and produce one diagnostic per failed callback and type.

Structural types can supply `PipeAppearance.interior` as a `PipeInteriorProfile`. Its `Members` property exposes a read-only snapshot of 1–16 immutable `PipeInteriorMember` objects. Each member specifies a finite `Vector2` offset, an immutable `PipeInteriorSection`, and either the `Core` or `Fittings` surface role. `Circle` accepts a radius fraction in `[0.01, 0.85]`. `RoundedRectangle` accepts full width and height fractions in `[0.02, 1.7]`, with a corner radius from zero through half the smaller side. Dimensions are normalized by the outer pipe radius. The envelope constraint is `offset.magnitude + section.RadiusFraction < 0.9`; rectangular sections use half their diagonal as a conservative enclosing radius. At shared endpoints, X follows the path tangent crossed with radial up, and Y follows radial up projected into the section plane. A profile enables `HasCore` automatically. With an omitted profile, `hasCore` and `coreRadiusFraction` retain the single-conductor behavior. The public construction example is in [Pipe types and appearance](PIPE_TYPES.md#内部纵向构件).

Directed sections follow native input-to-output orientation and may be asymmetric. Undirected profiles match members one-to-one at `(x, y)` and `(-x, y)`, including section kind, width, height, corner radius and surface role. Repeated members require equal reflected multiplicity. Path reversal flips X while radial Y retains its orientation. Type construction validates this symmetry, allowing a lower deck with distinct upper clearance. Longitudinal members follow the actual path and use shared endpoint tangents and radial frames, including across spherical segments. Rounded rectangles sweep flat sidewalls and circular corner arcs, with planar caps at free ends. Branch nodes generate one section per arm. Node geometry caches include type identity, endpoint positions, frames, fitting ownership and directed interior orientation. Topology and type changes invalidate the affected geometry; animation updates material properties. Interior submesh 0 is drawn with `Core`, and submesh 1 is drawn with `Fittings`; empty surfaces are skipped.

`PipeWallMode.Open` requires `Structure` behavior and an interior profile. Its render batches contain the configured members, with wall, inner-wall and outer-cap geometry omitted. Selection silhouettes include the visible `Core` and `Fittings` members. Collision bounds continue to use the configured geometry envelope. Open profiles can combine lateral box beams, a lower deck and conductors while retaining a clear central channel.

A closed circular structure can use a periodic world-position mapping to keep longitudinal textures continuous across chunk boundaries. For circumference `C` and desired world repeat length `L`, an extension can choose `N = max(1, round(C / L))`, map longitude to `atan2(z, x) / (2π) * P`, and set `textureCoordinatePeriod = P`. The renderer unwraps adjacent sections to the nearest phase. The extension applies a longitudinal texture scale of `N / P`, giving an actual repeat length of `C / N`; an integer repeat count closes the full-circle seam. All batches in that circle use the same circumference and mapping. Animation converts world speed to texture cycles per second by dividing by `C / N`.

Circumferential U follows normalized perimeter distance. Circular sections use uniform angular steps; rounded rectangles accumulate straight-edge lengths and rounded-corner arc lengths, keeping texture density consistent across both surfaces. Seam vertices share positions at U = 0 and U = 1, keeping interpolation local to each side. Planar end caps use a separate planar projection.

API v2 defines types through `PipeTypeDefinition.CreateSharedInventory` or `CreateStructure`, with dimensions supplied through the named `geometry:` parameter. A single `PipeAppearance` constructor configures materials, interior members, wall bands, texture coordinates and `receiveShadows`. Shadow reception defaults to true. Setting `receiveShadows: false` retains material lighting and shadow casting onto other geometry, which is useful when a simplified shadow proxy does not coincide with thin visible members. The option leaves global light shadow bias unchanged.

Optional direct machine ports use a procedural rectangular casing with a beveled border, recessed front panel and short circular pipe socket. Shared perimeter sections keep the casing opening and socket coaxial with the pipe, including elevated approaches. Four modeled fasteners sit on the front border. The shell, border and socket neck use a white dielectric surface with moderate smoothness. A charcoal inset panel and silver retaining rim define the opening. All opaque surfaces share the cached spatial chunk mesh.

Completed liquid inserters retain the native fixed endpoint housing mesh and UV coordinates for their grade. Its static material bakes the native alpha paint mask and grade tint into a cached texture; white and gray frame surfaces retain their authored colors. The Standard shader receives the metal/smoothness map, shared environmental reflection and a subtle neutral emission contribution for shadow visibility. The pipe saddle spans 0.29 world units along the pipe, with a 0.165-radius branch socket. Procedural grade bands read the native body material's `_Color` and use dielectric paint. Direction markers occupy a separate unlit submesh and read the native arrow material's `_TintColor`. Marker intensity follows the native power consumer's network supply ratio: an unpowered marker retains a readable grade tint, while full supply produces an HDR light signal. Supply changes update a material property block and preserve cached geometry. White dielectric support struts join the endpoint frame to an external collar around the upper transfer tube. The struts and collar remain within the tube assembly's height envelope. Metal tube sections and retaining rings, transparent tube walls, charcoal motor surfaces, grade paint, white supports and direction markers occupy separate material submeshes. Curved tube ends use explicit tangents matching the adjoining straight sections to keep their seam rings perpendicular to the shared centerline.

Inserter construction previews and pending buildings use the native inserter model, hologram materials and GPU instance lifecycle. Procedural tube rendering starts after construction completes. The scene fixture checks native preview ownership over 60 rendered frames and captures matching powered and unpowered views. Runtime arrow tests render all four grades against a dark background and measure visibility and supply-dependent brightness.

Completed inserter clamps resolve their center and tangent from the pipe renderer's cached node shape. Native cargo-path anchors project onto the visible straight segment or sampled elbow centerline; branch attachment selects the nearest arm with a stable tangent tie-break. Registered radius and center height determine the saddle and neck. Inventory updates reuse these shapes. Native path construction initializes missing preview poses from the route tangent; pending grid points and dismantle markers use a separate radial frame.
